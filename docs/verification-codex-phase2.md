## Boundaries retained

Observed contribution is not a business cause. Scenario and decision outputs depend
on declared formulas and assumptions. An unusual observation is not proof of fraud,
an error or a fault. Forecast error bands are not established calibrated probability
intervals. Cell grants authorize visible cells and do not create an anonymity system.
Real webhook delivery, public embedding/HTTPS, external identity, production security,
representative model quality and customer-scale performance remain independently gated.
